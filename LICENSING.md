## When you do **not** need a commercial license

You may use Afterburner in production with no separate agreement if you are:

- An individual building personal projects.
- A student using it for coursework.
- A non-commercial open-source project with no paid sponsorship, no monetized
  hosting, and no enterprise SLA.
- Anyone evaluating, developing, or testing internally (non-production), or
  making any other non-production use.

These uses are explicitly welcome.

2. **Embed it.** Incorporate Afterburner, in whole or in part, into a

3. **Compete with it.** Use Afterburner to build, operate, or assist any

This applies equally to forks, soft forks, derivative works, repackagings,
rebrands, vendored copies, and embedded copies, irrespective of where the
source originates.

## How to obtain a commercial license

- Your company / legal entity name.
